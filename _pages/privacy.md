This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are visited and how the site is used. Google Analytics is **optional** and only loads if you select **Accept analytics** in the cookie notice.

If you accept, Google may collect information about your visit, including pages viewed, browser and device information, and analytics cookies. This information is processed by Google. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

You can decline analytics without affecting your access to the website. To change your choice later, use **Cookie settings** in the footer. Your choice is saved in your browser for up to six months. Advertising personalization is not enabled through this setup.

Google Analytics reports and data retention are managed in the Google Analytics account.
